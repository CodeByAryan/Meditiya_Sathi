Build a modern, production-ready full-stack web application called "Meditiya Sathi", the official digital platform for Meditiya Nagar.

This is NOT just a Ganesh Utsav website. It is a complete digital society management platform that helps residents stay connected throughout the year while becoming the official festival portal during Ganesh Utsav.

The website should have a premium, elegant, and modern design inspired by Apple, Airbnb, Stripe, and Notion while preserving Indian cultural aesthetics.
